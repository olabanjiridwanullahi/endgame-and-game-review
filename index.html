    Position code (FEN)
    FEN is the text code chess sites use for a position. Copy it to share a position, or paste one from Chess.com or Lichess analysis. Supported: K+B+N, K+B+B, K+R or K+Q against a lone king.

    Coach mode
    Balanced (default): every move is played and rated on the board (★ Best, ✓ Good, ?! Inaccuracy, ? Mistake, ?? Blunder). Mistakes get a one-line reason with optional Retry and Show best buttons. Strict: mistakes are taken back until you find the best move. Off: no ratings during play, only in the review.
    Allowed extra moves (Strict mode)
    Strict mode only: accept moves that are only slightly slower than perfect. They still show as "?!" in the review.
